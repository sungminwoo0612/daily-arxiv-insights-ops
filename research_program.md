# Research Program

## Current Focus
- Retrieval-augmented generation quality
- Agentic workflows for research automation
- Research memory systems for personal knowledge compounding

## What To Prioritize
- Papers that improve retrieval quality, evaluation, or grounded synthesis
- Systems patterns that reduce self-hosting and maintenance burden
- Work that can be translated into reusable personal research workflows

## What To Deprioritize
- Generic benchmark papers without practical workflow implications
- Infra-heavy stacks that require always-on orchestration
- Features that optimize for team operations instead of personal leverage
